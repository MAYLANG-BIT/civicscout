FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

CivicScout is a crowdsourced web app for urban reporting where citizens act as Scouts to log municipal issues for LGU action.
You earn +50 XP when the LGU verifies your submitted report, +100 XP when it is resolved, and +15 XP for peer-confirming nearby open reports.
Select the "LGU Official" role when creating an account using your barangay authorization code, and you will be routed directly to the LGU Command Center.
